## Hello and welcome to the official l33chers Team blog!

Here we shall publish the HTB and CTF writeups for our past challenges and by best effort also the challenge files.

To sort the challenges by category we use the tags system so searching for forensics will find all of the forensics writeups for example.

Our ultimate goal is to educate for free. We are a open learning and CTFing community thriving by making it easy to get into the field of CTFing by not gatekeeping and encouraging curiosity.

We're active since the year 2020, we've been participating in many CTFs since then.

Our values are kindness, active support of each other and being down to earth.
